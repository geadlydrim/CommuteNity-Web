# Product Vision

## Elevator Pitch

CommuteNity is a community-powered navigation app for Filipino commuters. Where mainstream apps fall short on informal transit — jeepneys, tricycles, UV express, and provincial buses — CommuteNity fills the gap with crowd-sourced routes, fares, and real conditions shared by riders who use them daily. Anyone can look up a commute; anyone who knows better can improve it.

## Problem

Filipino commuting is fragmented: dozens of transit modes, few official schedules, fares that change without notice, and routes that exist mostly in the heads of long-time riders. Existing nav apps (Google Maps, Waze) cover highways and MRT but leave informal transit largely unmapped. Commuters — especially newcomers, provincial travelers, and balikbayans — frequently get lost, overpay, or board the wrong jeepney simply because no reliable reference exists.

## Solution

A mobile app where:
- **Riders find routes** — search by origin and destination, get a step-by-step commute plan across any combination of PH transit modes.
- **Community adds the knowledge** — every stop, route, fare, and condition note is crowd-sourced. No central authority maintains the data; the community does.
- **Trust is earned, not assumed** — upvotes, flags, and reputation signal which info is reliable.

## Target Personas

| Persona | Description | Core Need |
|---|---|---|
| **The Daily Rider** | Regular Metro Manila commuter; knows their own route cold but needs help with unfamiliar ones. | Quick, reliable route lookup with accurate current fares. |
| **The New Arrival** | Moves to a new city (student, OFW returnee, provincial transplant). No mental map of local transit yet. | Step-by-step guidance they can trust even without mobile data. |
| **The Knowledge Holder** | Long-time jeepney/bus rider who knows shortcuts, cheap fares, and seasonal schedule changes. | Easy way to contribute and correct wrong info; recognition for helping. |
| **The Occasional Commuter** | Drives usually, commutes only when forced. Unfamiliar with routes, intimidated by informal transit. | Simple interface; no transit jargon; clear "where do I board" instructions. |

## MVP Geography

> **ASSUMPTION — confirm with owner:** Initial launch focuses on **Metro Manila** (NCR). Data coverage and complexity are highest there, and the early user base is most concentrated. Provincial expansion in a later sprint.

## Success Metrics (MVP)

- Community submits and approves at least 50 routes within first month of beta.
- Average route lookup returns a valid result for 80% of Metro Manila origin-destination pairs tested.
- Contribution error-correction cycle (flag → fix) completes within 48 hours on average.
